# Copy Notes

The public-facing copy leads with mood, fragrance, craft, botanicals, skin-loving oils, and ritual. “Cannabinoid-infused” is used as the preferred category language.

The current product names “Muscle Relieving Peppermint” and “Sinus Relief Eucalyptus” were polished to “Peppermint Reset” and “Open Air Eucalyptus” for the website while their legacy names can remain as internal formula/SKU references.

The site does not use unsupported customer counts, magazine features, fake reviews, or permanent fake markdown pricing.
