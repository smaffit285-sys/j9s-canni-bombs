## Main Character Bath

- Romantic Rose Petal
- Jasmine Flower
- Renew
- Strawberries & Cream
- Very Berries & Cream
- Very Very Berry
- Sweet Blueberry
- Orange Cream

The uneven distribution is intentional. Main Character Bath is currently crowded with berry and sweet profiles, while Clocked Out has room for more sophisticated herbal, mineral, woody, ginger, rosemary, cypress, or salt-forward formulas.
