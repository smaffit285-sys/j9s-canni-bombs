Questions, answered

Before you drop in.

Clear answers help people buy confidently—and help search engines and answer tools understand exactly what the product is.

What does “cannabinoid-infused” mean?

Cannabinoids are incorporated into the bath-bomb formula alongside fragrance, botanicals, coconut oil, vitamin E, and the ingredients that create the fizzing bath experience. Each product is intended for external bath use.

Will a bath bomb make me feel intoxicated?

No. These are non-intoxicating bath products intended for external use. The experience comes from warm water, fragrance, botanicals, skin-loving oils, and the ritual of slowing down.

What is the difference between Ritual and Immersion?

Ritual is the smaller $11 option for trying a scent or gifting a little luxury. Immersion is the full-size $20 statement bomb with more product and presence.

Which collection should I choose?

Choose Clocked Out for fresh, herbal, cooling, or citrus-forward scents; Do Not Disturb for lavender, chamomile, sandalwood, vanilla, and slower evening profiles; or Main Character Bath for florals, berries, cream notes, romance, gifting, and beautiful water.

Are the products handmade?

Yes. Each batch is mixed, formed, finished, and packed in small quantities. Slight variations in color and botanical placement are part of the handmade character.

How should I store bath bombs in Florida humidity?

Keep them sealed, dry, and away from steam until bath time. A cool cabinet or drawer outside the bathroom is ideal if the room stays humid.
